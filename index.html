THE PASSDINNER RUSH

NIGHT 1BANK $0

Four stations, a printer that will not stop, and a career one service long — until you survive it. You are the whole line.

FIRE — click an item on a ticket to drop it in a pan on its station.
PULL — hit the pan's key inside the green window. Early is raw, late is burnt. Both cost you a re-fire.
PLATE — a ticket goes out when every item is pulled. Plates score on their coldest component — time your pulls together.
WEEDS — six tickets on the rail and the room turns on you until you dig out.
CAREER — tips bank between nights. Spend the bank on the line: more pans, heat lamps, a prep cook. Deeper nights hit harder: VIPs, bus tours, stations going 86'd, and the critic.
