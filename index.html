SPIKE-1THE PASS

Friday night. Four stations, eight pans, and a printer that will not stop. You are the whole line.

FIRE — click an item on a ticket to drop it in a pan on its station.
PULL — hit the pan's number key inside the green window. Early is raw, late is burnt. Both cost you a re-fire.
PLATE — a ticket only goes out when every item is pulled. Items go cold on the pass — the plate scores on its coldest component, so time your pulls together.
WEEDS — six tickets on the rail and the room turns on you: patience drains faster until you dig out to three.

Tickets get bigger and land harder as the night goes. Serve the room, don't burn the ribeye.
